What Does It Actually Do?

2 Precision Autonomous Jobs Shipped to Your Codebase Every Morning

AI crawlers do not read websites like humans; they look for structured Q&A blocks, valid Schema.org graphs, concise zero-click statements, and machine-readable data files like /llms.txt.

Answer Index runs automatically every day on your brand's preferred schedule, diagnoses what questions buyers are asking AI bots about your industry, and delivers two concrete pull requests to your GitHub repository:

  • Job 1 (New Answer Piece): Creates one net-new authoritative direct-answer page targeting an uncovered commercial search prompt.
  • Job 2 (Technical Refactor): Rewrites and optimizes an existing slipping page, injecting JSON-LD schema, anti-hallucination ground truth statements, and latency optimizations.
🛡️ Zero Risk / Human-in-the-Loop: Nothing deploys to your site automatically. You inspect the code diff and zero-click answer excerpt, then click "1-Click Approve" on your dashboard or merge on GitHub.
