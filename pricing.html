Frequently Asked Questions

Transparent answers before you start.

Can Answer Index publish without my explicit approval? ▾
Never. Answer Index operates on a strict human-in-the-loop philosophy. Every piece of work is delivered as a 1-click review draft in your chosen platform—WordPress draft queue, Shopify article, Web Portal, or GitHub Pull Request. You inspect the copy, check the Schema validator score, and choose when to approve.
What is the Brand Truth Wiki and how does it prevent AI hallucinations? ▾
We store an immutable ground-truth fact document directly in Cloudflare D1 per project. Before drafting any asset, our pre-flight Anti-Hallucination Gate checks all claims against your official wiki. Outdated pricing or false feature claims are deterministically filtered out before staging.
Are there contracts, setup fees, or cancellation penalties? ▾
None. All plans are billed month-to-month with zero setup fees. You can cancel at any time with a single click inside your Client Dashboard. Every subscription is backed by our 14-day money-back guarantee.
Do I get access to founder strategy support? ▾
Yes! Pro Autonomous and Enterprise DFY subscriptions include direct strategy reviews with Sameer Sharma (creator of Answer Index). We review your citation growth across Perplexity and ChatGPT, analyze Google Search Console trends, and tune your entity graph roadmap together.
