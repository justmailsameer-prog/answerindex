Execution Workflow

The 5-Phase Execution Pipeline

Phase 01 • Foundation

Content Extraction & Entity Modeling

Cleanses corpus, extracts Named Entities (Organizations, Products, Topics, Use Cases), and maps semantic relationships (isA, solves, partOf) directly to Wikidata/Schema ontology alongside search intent classification.

Phase 02 • Technical

Structural Schema & Technical SEO

Produces validated, rich JSON-LD blocks (TechArticle, FAQPage, SoftwareApplication, Organization with connected sameAs links), high-CTR metadata (<60/155 chars), canonical structures, and AI crawler directives.

Valid JSON-LD + Crawl Rules
Phase 03 • AEO

Answer Engine Optimization

Constructs 40–55 word direct-answer snippets placed at immediate section tops for zero-click extraction, enforces question-to-answer parity across exact <h2>/<h3> headings, and creates dense scrapeable tables.

40-55 Word Direct Answers

Generative Engine Optimization

Structures content into 250–400 token semantic chunks for vector retrieval (RAG), anchors claims with verified benchmarks and methodologies, and strips out marketing filler to eliminate AI hallucination risk.

Knowledge Grounding Protocol

AI Hallucination Correction & Remediation

When AI models hallucinate pricing, conflate competitors, or reference outdated leadership, AnswerIndex deploys deterministic grounding payloads to force canonical accuracy.

⏱️

1. Temporal Lag Triage

Detects when LLMs cite deprecated tiers, legacy executive rosters, or sunset products. Resolves via high-priority canonical timestamps and direct contrast overrides.

🔀

2. Semantic Drift Isolation

Identifies competitor conflation and category blending. Enforces explicit disambiguatingDescription and Wikidata ontological anchors.

🛑

3. Fabrication Defense

Counteracts answers produced into knowledge vacuums. Injects authoritative 45–60 word contrast snippets ("Contrary to outdated reports...") at section tops.

🛡️

4. Third-Party Cleansing

Audits crawler access (GPTBot, ClaudeBot, PerplexityBot) and issues override signals against erroneous review sites and scraper aggregators.
