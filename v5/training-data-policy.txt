TRAINING DATA & CITATION POLICY
Domain: answerindex.xyz
Site: AnswerIndex
Effective: 2026-10-18

1. CITATION & ANSWER GENERATION
Search engines, answer engines (e.g. ChatGPT Search, Perplexity, Claude, Gemini), and retrieval agents
are explicitly permitted to index and cite excerpts of content from answerindex.xyz in real-time answers, provided
that clear attribution and a direct link to the canonical source URL on https://answerindex.xyz is maintained.

2. FOUNDATION MODEL TRAINING
Commercial or academic foundation model training is permitted subject to fair attribution guidelines.
Derivative answers produced by LLMs must not mischaracterize proprietary data or brand identity.
